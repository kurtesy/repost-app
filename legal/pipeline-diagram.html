Historical repost tool

How one post moves through the pipeline

Six stages, from the original Instagram reel to a logged result on YouTube or TikTok. Each stage highlights in turn.

01 — source

Instagram

Fetch historical reels and video posts via the Graph API.

02 — storage

Local media table

Video file, caption, and parsed hashtags saved to the local database.

03 — selection

Workflow picks a post

Manually checked rows, or the next un-posted item in newest- or oldest-first order.

04 — mapping

Caption and tag mapping

One caption becomes a YouTube title/description/tags, or a TikTok caption with hashtags inline.

05 — dedupe

Duplicate check

Cross-checked against this app's own post log, and — for YouTube — the channel's real upload history.

06 — delivery

YouTube Shorts

Uploaded with title, description, tags.

TikTok

Posted, or drafted if unaudited.
